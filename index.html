Training paces

SessionBased onPaceRep targets

Each pace is a range: the faster figure is the most you should push, and the slower end is fine on tired days or in heat and wind. Sub-threshold reps should feel controlled, and you should finish every session feeling you could have done another rep. If the effort climbs, slow down. Heart rate and lactate matter more than the pace on your watch.

Plan settings

Volume rises at most 5% a week over the last full week. Lighter weeks drop to 80%, then the build resumes from where it left off. Sub-T rep counts are sized from each week's volume, so sessions grow as volume grows. Warm-up and cool-down apply to every sub-T and 400s / hills session.

Weekly template

Classic layout: 2–3 sub-threshold days with an easy day between each, plus one long easy run. Most of your weekly volume should be easy running.

Connect intervals.icu

Connect your intervals.icu account to send planned workouts from the 12-Week Builder. They then sync from intervals.icu to your watch (COROS, Garmin and others) if you've connected it there.

Sending to intervals.icu doesn't work in this preview, because it can't connect to other websites. Open the hosted planner at haji-s13.github.io/NSM to connect your account and send workouts.

How sending works

  • On the 12-Week Builder, use Send on a day, Send week, or Send all to intervals.icu.
  • Workouts arrive as structured runs with pace ranges: warm-up, reps with recoveries, cool-down.
  • Sending a day again replaces that day's planner workout instead of adding a duplicate.
  • Leave Athlete ID blank to use the account the API key belongs to.

Your key is only sent to intervals.icu. If you don't tick "Remember", it's forgotten when you close the page.
